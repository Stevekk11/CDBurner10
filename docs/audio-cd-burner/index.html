How to Burn an Audio CD with CD-Text

  1. Open Audio Compilation
    Launch CDBurner10 and click Audio Compilation in the navigation pane.
  2. Add tracks or import playlists
    Drag and drop audio files (decoded via NAudio, TagLibSharp, and optional FFmpeg for exotic codecs) or import .cue, .m3u, .wpl, and .nra playlists. Artist and title tags automatically populate CD-Text fields.
  3. Choose SAO/DAO-96 for gapless playback & CD-Text
    Select SAO/DAO-96 (Session/Disc-At-Once) to write CD-Text and eliminate 2-second gaps between live album tracks. Optionally enable ReplayGain volume leveling or smooth crossfading.
  4. Burn your CD-R
    Insert a blank CD-R, verify the disc capacity bar (up to 80 minutes on standard 700 MB CD-R), and click Burn.

Ripping Audio CDs to Your PC

Built-in CD-DA Ripper

Insert any Audio CD and open the Rip Audio CD dialog to extract tracks directly via Win32 CD-DA IOCTLs using Export Tracks to... — no extra ripping software needed.
